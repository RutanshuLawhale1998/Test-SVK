Nukad Coffee is a corner. Two benches, one urn, a shutter that comes down at nobody's particular hour. People turn up for a cup and stay for the argument.

The tape recorder has been on the counter since before the coffee was good. It plays audio only — no screens at this corner — and it runs six rotations depending on what the hour deserves. Pick one and it loads. Nothing to paste, nothing to sign into.
